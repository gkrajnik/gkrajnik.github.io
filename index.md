## Curiosity, backed by experiments.

My interests center on mechanistic interpretability: understanding the computations behind a model's behavior. My work spans language-model representations during roleplay and small recurrent networks performing algorithmic tasks.

I use Python, PyTorch, and data visualization to turn research questions into experiments, working across local and university GPU computing environments.
